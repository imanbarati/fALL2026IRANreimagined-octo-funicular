# Atlas Entry Template

Every subject in `atlas/registry/*.csv` eventually becomes one entry in an Atlas chapter, using this shape. Entries are **appended**, never rewritten. Corrections go in `ERRATA.md` or a dated addendum entry.

```
### [ID] Title (Year or span)

**Type:** event | law | program | institution | treaty
**Jurisdiction:** country or body
**Date(s):** exact dates where known
**What happened:** 2–5 plain sentences, no adjectives that carry the argument.
**Who was affected:** groups, numbers if sourced.
**Why it matters for recovery:** 1–3 sentences linking to a book chapter.
**Sources:** at least one primary or reputable secondary source (URL + access date).
**Verification:** VERIFIED | PARTLY VERIFIED | VERIFY
**Related chapters / IDs:** ch09, A-0042, E-0013
```

Rules: no invented numbers, quotes or documents; quote under 15 words; paraphrase; name sources; separate allegation from finding; criticize governments and policies, never peoples.
