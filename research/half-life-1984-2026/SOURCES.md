## Verification warnings
- Do not treat Iranian, US, Israeli, opposition or armed-group statements as independently verified facts.
- Keep separate counts for killed, injured, detained, disappeared, executed and missing.
- For the 1988 prison executions, state that totals vary and explain the source and method used.
- For protest episodes, date each estimate and identify whether the source counts confirmed names, reported deaths or broader estimates.
- For the 2026 war, specify whether a statement is a parliamentary briefing, UN finding, official claim or independent report.
- For economic data, distinguish calendar year from Iranian fiscal year, nominal from real values, market from official exchange rates, and projections from final outcomes.
- For the nuclear file, distinguish IAEA safeguards findings, JCPOA compliance, Security Council obligations and political allegations.
