# Chapter 7 — Rebuilding the State and Civil Society

Iran's state is not just a building in Tehran. It is a thousand offices, courts, clinics, municipal councils and unions. After years of repression and war, many are hollow: staffed by people who are underpaid, afraid or absent.

## The state that serves

- **Professional civil service.** Merit-based hiring, public salary scales, and rules that stop a ministry from being a family business.
- **Security forces under civilian control.** Police and army answer to law; intelligence services operate under warrants and parliamentary oversight; no security body owns commercial enterprises.
- **Local government.** Councils with budgets and real powers over schools, water, roads and local policing.
- **Independent institutions.** An election commission, an audit court, a human-rights commission, a central bank, a statistics office, none removable at the whim of the executive.

## The society that holds the state accountable

A state without civil society is a monologue. Rebuilding requires:

- **Free press and open internet.** Licensing without political tests; legal protection for journalists; no shutdowns.
- **Unions and professional associations.** Teachers, nurses, truckers and bazaar guilds negotiating freely.
- **Universities.** Academic freedom, return of dismissed faculty, open admissions.
- **Women's and youth organizations.** Chapter 16 will examine these in depth.
- **Religious and cultural communities.** Free to organize and worship, equally protected (see Chapter 13).
- **Non-governmental organizations.** Registration by notice, not permission.

## Federalism, decentralization, unity

Citizens in Kurdistan, Baluchestan, Azerbaijan, Khuzestan, Turkmen Sahra and elsewhere hold different views about autonomy. We do not decide that for them. Chapter 17 sets out the strongest arguments for a unitary state and for federalism, and the coalition will support whichever structure Iranians choose in a fair vote.

## Objections and Honest Limits

*"Civil society cannot be rebuilt quickly."* True; trust returns slowly. But institutions can be protected by law from day one.

*"A strong state is safer than a free one."* A strong state without checks produced the present situation. A strong state with checks is the aim.

## What We Will Do / What You Can Do

- Support organizations inside Iran that survived repression, quietly and safely.
- Publish model charters for unions, NGOs and municipal councils.
