## 1. What this project is
An ebook credited to **National Iran Activists Coalition** about national rejuvenation and recovery after the war and after the damage done by the Islamic Republic: how to undo the damage and rebuild Iran. Working title: **Iran Reborn: A National Recovery Manual**. Advocacy voice (the coalition's), but evidence-based and honest about risks.

Related earlier project by the same owner: https://github.com/imanbarati/effective-carnival

1. **Append-only: ALWAYS ADD NEW CHAPTERS, NEVER REWRITE EXISTING CHAPTERS.** Do not edit, shorten, reorder or renumber published chapter files. Corrections go in `ERRATA.md` or in a new chapter/addendum. New chapters take the next number.
2. **Never ask the owner questions.** Make the best decision, note it in the Decision Log, and continue.
3. **Byline is the coalition only.** No AI attribution inside the manuscript files.
4. Manuscript is English first; Persian edition later in `/fa/`.
5. Keep this HANDOFF.md current: update the status table after every chapter you add.

## 3. Style and quality guide
- Voice: serious, urgent, humane; "we" = the coalition. Criticize governments/institutions/policies, never peoples or religions.
- Each chapter ~1,200–2,500 words; opening hook; claims; proposals; "Objections and Honest Limits"; "What We Will Do / What You Can Do".
- Every factual number gets a source or `[VERIFY]` tag. Do not invent statistics.
- Do not write the closing "Call to Action" until the owner declares the book complete.
