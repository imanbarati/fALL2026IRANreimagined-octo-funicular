# Chapter 23 — Preface to Atlas Part F: World Laws and Programs on Migration, Labor, Environment, Space, Sea, Religion and State, Information and Corruption

*Entries live in `atlas/registry/part-F-world-laws-and-programs.csv`.*

The later chapters of this book ask questions that Iran shares with the whole world: how to treat refugees and skilled migrants, how to end child labor in supply chains, how to share rivers and seas, how to govern space and the internet, how to separate religion from state without persecuting believers, and how to stop corruption. Part F gathers the treaties, statutes and programs that governments have tried.

## What a subject is in Part F

One law, treaty, regulation or national program, with its date, jurisdiction, aims, results and critics.

## Groups in Part F

Migration and asylum · child labor and mining · conflict minerals and corporate due diligence · space law · maritime law and straits · climate, water and wetlands · population policies (pronatalist and family planning) · religion and state · information freedom and surveillance · anti-corruption bodies · constitutions and human-rights treaties.

## How the part connects to the chapters

- Chapter 9 (exodus and return) draws on migration and skilled-visa programs.
- Chapter 11 (population) draws on population laws of China, Romania, Hungary and Iran itself.
- Chapter 12 (planet) draws on water, wetland and climate agreements, including the Ramsar Convention, signed in Iran in 1971.
- Chapter 13 (religion) draws on secularism laws from France, Turkey, India, Mexico and the United States.
- Chapter 15 (world) draws on the child-labor, cobalt, space and sea-law entries.

## Method

Entries describe what a law says and what is known of its effect, not whether it is morally ideal. Both successes and failures are listed, including population policies that caused harm.

## Objections and Honest Limits

*"These laws are from very different countries."* Yes; the point is to see the range of options.

*"Some are weakly enforced."* Many are. The entries will say so.
