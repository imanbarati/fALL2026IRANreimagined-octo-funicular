# Chapter 20 — Preface to Atlas Part C: Programs and Institutions of the Iranian State

*Entries live in `atlas/registry/part-C-iran-state-programs.csv`.*

Institutions outlast leaders. The Revolutionary Guard, the Basij, the bonyads, Setad, the Ministry of Intelligence, the Supreme Council of Cyberspace and the National Information Network shape daily life more than most statutes do. Part C lists the programs and bodies through which the Iranian state governs, enforces, taxes, trades and projects power abroad.

## What a subject is in Part C

One institution or program: when it began, what it does, who funds it, who oversees it, and what it costs citizens. Programs of the state that served people well, such as vaccination and rural electrification drives, are listed alongside programs that harmed them.

## Why this matters for recovery

A transition must decide what to do with each institution: dissolve, reform, merge, or keep. A security force that is merely renamed will behave as before. An economic holding that is seized without auditors will vanish into private hands. Part C is the inventory.

## Categories

Security and intelligence · economy and holdings · information and internet control · culture and education · religion and seminary finance · women and public morality · proxies and external operations · military and missiles · population and welfare · reconstruction.

## Method and caution

Many entries rely on reporting from Iranian state media, Iranian opposition media, foreign governments and independent researchers, all of which have interests. The registry therefore records the *source type* for each claim. Allegations of external operations are marked as allegations unless courts or international bodies have found otherwise.

## Objections and Honest Limits

*"Institutions are not monolithic."* Correct: individuals inside them have acted with courage and with cruelty. This part describes structures, not every person in them.

*"The economic holdings are secret."* Largely true. That is itself a finding.
